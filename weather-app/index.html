Everything you need before you step outside

Real-time weather

Current temperature, feels-like, wind, humidity, pressure, visibility and UV, straight from the forecast model.

Hourly forecasts

A scrollable timeline with the temperature curve, chance of rain and wind for the next day or two.

Seven-day forecasts

Highs and lows drawn on a shared scale, so you can compare the week at a glance.

Worldwide location search

Find any city on Earth. Places that share a name show their region and country so you pick the right one.

Favourite locations

Save the places you care about and see their conditions together on one page.

Weather insights

Charts for temperature trends, precipitation chances and wind, built from the same live data.

Personalized settings

Choose Celsius or Fahrenheit, your wind unit, a default location and a light, dark or system theme. Your choices follow you across devices.

About OsasWeather

OsasWeather is a weather platform built for clarity: the current conditions come first, and everything else is arranged so it is easy to read on a phone, a laptop or a large screen.

Forecast data and place search are provided by Open-Meteo.com under the CC BY 4.0 licence. When data is not available we show that it is not available, never a made-up number.
